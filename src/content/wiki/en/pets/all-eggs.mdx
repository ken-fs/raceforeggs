Eggs are Race for Eggs' loot containers, and understanding them is the difference between grinding randomly and farming with intent. This guide covers every egg type we could verify, where each one comes from, and the carrying rule that decides which courses are actually farmable.

## How do eggs work?

The official loop is short: **train to gain Speed, win Races to earn Eggs**. Every egg in the game traces back to that sentence:

- **Racing is the only repeatable source.** Shop tiers sell eggs directly, but racing scales with your speed while the shop scales with your wallet.
- **Placement matters more than finishing.** The egg is the reward for winning — second place does not pay the same.
- **Carrying an egg slows you down.** Your usable speed is the speed that wins *while holding the egg*. This single rule decides everything about farming (below).

## What egg types exist?

| Egg | Source | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Basic egg** | Starting pool | Your first hatches — the tutorial-grade container |
| **Sphinx egg** | Shop tier 4 and up | Sold alongside the Rainbow Dog and Snake at higher tiers |
| **Turkey egg** | Shop tier 4 and up | Same tier as the sphinx egg |
| **T-Rex egg** | Later worlds | Seen in world two during creator runs |
| **Magma dragon egg** | Higher-tier content | A step up from the standard pools |
| **Secret eggs** | End-game | Holds the rarest pets in the game |

The roster shifts with the **Saturday updates** — new eggs are exactly the kind of content a weekly patch adds, so treat this table as the verified September set rather than a final list.

## Why the carrying rule matters for farming

**Carrying an egg slows you down** — so the course you can farm is not the course you can run fastest, it is the hardest course you can win *with an egg in hand*. That produces two practical strategies:

1. **Farm down, not up.** A course you win reliably while carrying beats a flashier course where you place second. Consistency compounds; hero runs do not.
2. **Re-run familiar tracks.** A rare egg can turn up on a course you already clear comfortably. Experienced players keep re-running easy tracks precisely because the egg roll does not care how hard the course was.

The corollary: when you stall, the fix is usually **speed**, not a harder course — and speed is what [training](/guides/speed-guide/) buys.

## Where do worlds fit in?

The game is structured into **worlds** (world one, world two, and beyond), and each world gates its own courses and egg pools — the T-Rex egg, for example, shows up in world two. Progressing a world unlocks:

- New courses with their own egg rolls
- New shop stock at higher tiers
- Access to the egg types that gate stronger pets

Don't rush the world progression at the cost of farming efficiency: a player who farms world one consistently will out-earn a player who barely scrapes through world two.

## What about secret eggs?

**Secret eggs hold the rarest pets** — the Kraken, dragon eggs, and the rest of the end-game chase. The community's biggest videos are built around them, which tells you two things: they are the highest-payoff content in the game, and they are not where a new account should start.

The sequencing that works: build a cash engine with common pets ([pets guide](/pets/all-pets/)), push speed so harder egg rolls become winnable, and take [rebirths](/guides/rebirth-guide/) whenever the grind slows. Secrets come last — and they come much faster once the first two steps are done.
