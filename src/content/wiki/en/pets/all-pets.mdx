Race for Eggs pets follow a strict loop: **win races → earn eggs → hatch pets → pets earn cash → cash buys speed → speed wins races**. Understanding that circle is most of the game. This guide covers where pets come from, how levels and mutations change their value, and where the secret pets sit in the chase.

## Where do pets come from?

**Winning races is the only repeatable egg source.** The official description is explicit — you train to gain Speed and win Races to earn Eggs. That single rule shapes everything:

- **Placement matters more than finishing.** The egg is the reward for winning, so a course you can win beats a harder course you can't.
- **Carrying an egg slows you down.** Your usable speed is the speed that wins *while holding the egg* — a course becomes farmable only once you can clear it with the egg in hand.
- **Rare eggs can appear on easy courses.** A rare egg can turn up on a track you already clear comfortably, so re-running a familiar course is a real strategy, not a waste.

Beyond racing, the launch code **RELEASE** grants one free **Lizard pet** ([codes page](/codes/all-codes/)), and the shop sells consumables (cash potions, training potions) plus exclusive packs that appear in tiers. The shop sells acceleration — racing remains the source.

## How do pets earn money?

**Placed pets earn cash over time.** That income is what pays for speed training and shop upgrades, which is why the pet economy compounds:

| Step | What it buys |
| --- | --- |
| Pets earn cash (passive, every session) | — |
| Cash buys training + potions | Faster Speed growth |
| Speed wins harder races | Rarer eggs |
| Rarer eggs hatch better pets | Bigger cash income |

Two details worth knowing early:

- **Pet slots are limited** — creator videos show slot counts being upgraded with cash, so part of your early budget goes to fitting more pets on the board.
- **Roblox Plus accounts earn +10% cash** — the official description states it, and over a long session that compounds with everything else.

## What are mutations — and why do duplicates matter?

**Mutations are named variants that change what a pet is worth.** Creator footage shows a radioactive mammoth and gold variants — one creator landed a gold mutation described as a **1% chance**, which gives a sense of how rare the good rolls are. The practical consequence: two copies of the same pet are not the same asset, so check the variant before you sell or trade one away.

Pets also **level up to a maximum** — creator videos show max-level pet unlocks — so duplicates feed progression rather than being dead weight.

## What are the secret pets?

Secret pets come from secret eggs and sit at the **top of the end-game chase**. The most-watched Race for Eggs creator content covers secret eggs and max-level secret pets — a Kraken unlock, a dragon egg secret, radioactive mammoths — which is a reliable signal of where the community's attention (and the biggest payoffs) live.

For a new player the takeaway is sequencing: don't chase secrets first. Build the cash engine with common pets, then let speed and rare-egg luck carry you toward them. The [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) lays out that first path, and the [rebirth guide](/guides/rebirth-guide/) explains the multiplier that makes the whole loop faster.
