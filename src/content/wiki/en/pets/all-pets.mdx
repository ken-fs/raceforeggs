Race for Eggs pets follow a strict loop: **win races → earn eggs → hatch pets → pets earn cash → cash buys speed → speed wins races**. This guide covers every pet and egg we could verify from creator coverage and official material, how mutations change a pet's value, and where the secret pets sit in the chase.

## What pets are in the game?

The roster grows with the **Saturday updates**, so treat this as the verified set rather than the final list. Names seen across creator coverage:

| Pet | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Kraken** | A secret-tier chase pet — creator videos are built around unlocking it at max level |
| **Sphinx** | Sold as an egg at higher shop tiers (the sphinx egg) |
| **T-Rex** | Its own egg type, encountered in the second world |
| **Moth Man** | A "special" pet seen in creator pulls |
| **Rainbow Dog** | Stocked at the shop's tier 4 alongside turkey and sphinx eggs |
| **Snake** | Also sold directly at higher shop tiers |
| **Seahorse** | A shop purchase used in progression runs |
| **Bronchiosaurus** | Seen in creator pulls — and in a radioactive variant |
| **Lizard** | The free pet from the RELEASE code ([codes page](/codes/all-codes/)) |

The pattern worth internalizing: **pets come from three places** — racing (the engine), shop tiers (paid acceleration), and codes (free starters). Only racing scales indefinitely.

## What egg types exist?

Eggs are the containers, and their quality gates what you can hatch:

| Egg | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **Basic egg** | The starting pool — your first hatches |
| **Sphinx egg** | Shop tier 4 and up |
| **Turkey egg** | Shop tier 4 and up |
| **T-Rex egg** | Later worlds (seen in world two) |
| **Magma dragon egg** | Higher-tier content |
| **Secret eggs** | The end-game chase — rarer pets than anything else |

Two mechanical rules shape egg hunting:

1. **Carrying an egg slows you down.** Your usable speed is the speed that wins *while holding the egg* — a course becomes farmable only once you can clear it with the egg in hand.
2. **Rare eggs can appear on easy courses.** A rare egg can turn up on a track you already clear comfortably, so re-running a familiar course is a real strategy, not a waste.

The full egg breakdown — sources, gating, and farming routes — is in the [eggs guide](/pets/all-eggs/).

## How do mutations work?

**Mutations are named variants that change what a pet is worth.** Creator footage shows:

- **Radioactive** — a radioactive Bronchiosaurus was pulled on camera
- **Gold** — one creator landed a gold mutation and described it as a **1% chance**, which gives a sense of the rarity curve
- **Rainbow** — rainbow variants and a "rainbow dice" shop item appear in footage, consistent with the game's rainbow/colorful theme

The practical consequence: **two copies of the same pet are not the same asset.** Before selling or trading a duplicate, check its variant — a mutated common can out-earn a plain rare.

## Do pets level up?

**Yes — pets level to a maximum**, and creator videos show max-level pet unlocks. Levelling runs alongside hatching: your existing pets get stronger while you chase new ones, which is why duplicates feed progression rather than being dead weight. Pet slots are limited and upgradable, so part of your cash budget goes to fitting more earners on the board.

## Where do the secret pets sit?

Secret pets from secret eggs are the **end-game chase**. The community's most-watched Race for Eggs videos are built around them — a max-level Kraken unlock, a dragon egg secret, radioactive pulls — which is a reliable signal of where the biggest payoffs live.

For new players the sequencing is simple: build the cash engine with common pets first, then let speed and rare-egg luck carry you toward secrets.
