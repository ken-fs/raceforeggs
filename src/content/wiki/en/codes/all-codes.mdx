One Race for Eggs code is working as of **September 19, 2026**: **RELEASE**, which grants a free **Lizard pet**. A code pet is not a shortcut past the game loop — it is a head start inside it. Pets earn cash over time, cash pays for speed training, and speed is what wins races. The Lizard starts that engine on your first session, before you have won anything.

## How do I redeem Race for Eggs codes?

Redemption happens in the **Shop menu**, not the main menu:

1. Open Race for Eggs on Roblox and wait for your character to finish loading.
2. Look for the **Shop button** on screen and open the menu.
3. Find the **redeem box** inside the shop window.
4. Paste the code **exactly as written** (codes are case-sensitive — copy-paste, don't type) and press **Redeem**.
5. Check your **pet list** for the reward — the Lizard appears there, not in your inventory.

**Redeem troubleshooting** — if RELEASE is rejected:

- **Rejoin a fresh server first.** The redeem box occasionally needs a new session after a patch, especially on update days.
- **"Invalid code"**: check for a trailing space from the copy, or a lowercase l read as a capital I.
- **"Already redeemed"**: each code works once per account.

## What is the Lizard pet worth?

Pets in Race for Eggs are the economic engine: placed pets earn cash over time, and that income funds everything else — speed training, shop upgrades, and the potions that accelerate both. A free pet is therefore worth more than a one-off cash grant of the same size, because it keeps paying every session.

Early on, the practical value is tempo: with the Lizard earning from minute one, you can afford your first speed upgrades without grinding early races. That compounds — faster training means winning races sooner, and winning races is the only repeatable way to earn eggs ([how the pet loop works](/pets/all-pets/)).
