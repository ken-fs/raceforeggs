Four Race for Eggs codes are working as of **October 11, 2026**: **TRAIN1**, **LUCK1** and **UPD1** — the potion codes added with the October patches — plus **RELEASE**, the launch code that grants a free **Lizard pet**. The list has grown from one code to four in three weeks, so check back after every Saturday update.

## What changed in October?

The game started shipping codes alongside its weekly patches, and three arrived in a single week:

- **TRAIN1** — first listed October 7, right after the weekend patch. Grants one potion.
- **LUCK1** — first listed October 7 as well. Grants one potion.
- **UPD1** — first listed October 3. Grants one potion.
- **RELEASE** — still working. It has been live since roughly September 17 and remains the only free pet code.

All four entries were cross-checked against three independent code trackers on **October 11, 2026**. If a code stops working, that tracker trail is how we catch it — and the comments are open for reports.

## How do I redeem Race for Eggs codes?

Redemption happens in the **Shop menu**, not the main menu:

1. Open Race for Eggs on Roblox and wait for your character to finish loading.
2. Look for the **Shop button** on screen and open the menu.
3. Find the **redeem box** inside the shop window.
4. Paste the code **exactly as written** (codes are case-sensitive — copy-paste, don't type) and press **Redeem**.
5. Check your **pet list** for the reward.

**Redeem troubleshooting** — if a code is rejected:

- **Rejoin a fresh server first.** The redeem box occasionally needs a new session after a patch, especially on update days.
- **"Invalid code"**: check for a trailing space from the copy, or a lowercase l read as a capital I.
- **"Already redeemed"**: each code works once per account.

## What is each reward worth?

**RELEASE — the Lizard pet.** Pets are the economic engine: placed pets earn cash over time, and that income funds speed training, shop upgrades, and potions. A free pet is worth more than a one-off cash grant of the same size, because it keeps paying every session. Early on the value is tempo: with the Lizard earning from minute one, you can afford your first speed upgrades without grinding early races ([how the pet loop works](/pets/all-pets/)).

**TRAIN1, LUCK1, UPD1 — potions.** These are accelerators, and the names map to the stats they boost: training, luck, and cash. Use them when the matching grind is your bottleneck — a training potion before a session where you are pushing speed, a cash potion once your pets are already earning. Exact multipliers are not published, so treat them as a one-session boost rather than a permanent upgrade.

## What should I do after redeeming?

Redeem all four codes, then follow the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) — the short version is: train speed, win a race while carrying an egg, hatch it, and let the Lizard plus your first hatched pet start paying for upgrades.
