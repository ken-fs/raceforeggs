Speed is the stat everything else in Race for Eggs funnels through: you **train to gain Speed**, and speed is what wins the races that earn the eggs that hatch the pets that pay for more speed. This guide covers how training works, what potions do, and the one rule that decides how much speed you actually need.

## How does speed training work?

The official loop puts it first: **train to gain Speed**. Training is a repeatable action, not a one-time purchase, and its rate is what potions multiply:

| Item | Effect | When to buy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Training potions** | Double your training rate | First — speed is the bottleneck that gates everything |
| **Cash potions** | Double your cash income | Once pets are earning well (income is the bottleneck) |
| **Pet slots** | More pets earning at once | Early — passive income compounds |
| **Exclusive packs** | Tiered bundles with pets/items | When a pack's contents fill a real gap |

The logic of the order: **training buys races, races buy eggs, eggs buy pets, pets buy cash**. Cash potions accelerate the last step; training potions accelerate the first. When in doubt, accelerate the first.

## How much speed do I actually need?

One number matters, and it is not your personal best:

> **Your usable speed is the speed that wins a race while carrying an egg.**

The game slows you down while you hold an egg — so a course you can win freely may be unwinnable with an egg in hand. That gives a clean training target:

- **Win a course empty-handed?** Good, but that's not the test.
- **Win it while carrying an egg?** That course is now farmable, and its egg rolls are yours.

When you stall, resist the urge to move to a harder course. Go back, farm the one you can win with an egg, and let the income fund the next speed tier. This is the single most common progression mistake in the game.

## What does the speed scale look like?

Creator footage from "starting over" runs shows the curve clearly:

- **Early game**: speed in the millions, races won on basic courses
- **Mid game**: speed in the billions (one creator run hit 8 billion while rebuilding)
- **Late game**: trillions and beyond — one late-game shop purchase in footage required 300 trillion

The scale is exponential, which explains the feel of the game: early training crawls, and late-game training jumps whole magnitudes at a time. **Rebirth is what bends the curve** — each one multiplies cash, and cash is training fuel. The [rebirth guide](/guides/rebirth-guide/) covers when to take them.

## What else affects race outcomes?

Three factors beyond raw speed:

1. **The carrying penalty** — covered above; it's the biggest one.
2. **Course familiarity** — a track you know beats a harder track you don't. Route knowledge is free speed.
3. **Competition** — servers hold a handful of players, and creator footage shows races decided by seconds. Racing at off-peak hours is a real (if unglamorous) strategy.

## What should I do next?

The efficient order of operations:

1. Redeem the [working codes](/codes/all-codes/) — the free Lizard pet starts your income floor.
2. Follow the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) through your first egg win.
3. Buy training potions until your carrying-speed wins, then push the next course.
4. Take [rebirths](/guides/rebirth-guide/) when the grind slows — they are the multiplier on this entire loop.

For what you are actually racing toward, see the [pets guide](/pets/all-pets/) and the [eggs guide](/pets/all-eggs/).
