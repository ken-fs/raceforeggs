Rebirth is Race for Eggs' compounding engine: you trade a run's progress for a **permanent cash multiplier**, then rebuild faster than before. Creator footage from a fresh-account session shows the multiplier climbing past **512× the base cash rate** — which is the reason late-game income numbers in this game look absurd (billions, even trillions of speed). This guide covers how the trade works and when to take it.

## What exactly does rebirth trade?

**Current progress for a permanent multiplier on cash earnings.** That is the whole deal. The reset is real — you rebuild your speed and income from a lower point — but the multiplier applies to everything you earn afterward, so the rebuilt state overtakes the old one quickly.

The evidence for how fast: in a "starting over" creator run, the player **rebirthed five times in quick succession** and later had multipliers running at **512× base cash**. Rebirthing repeatedly in one session is only rational if each rebuild is faster than the last — and with the multiplier climbing, it is.

## When should I rebirth?

The timing rule is simple to state and takes practice to feel:

> **Rebirth when rebuilding will be faster than continuing the current grind.**

What that looks like in practice:

| Situation | Take the rebirth? |
| --- | --- |
| Training is slowing down and the next upgrade is far away | ✅ Yes — the multiplier resets the pace |
| You just unlocked something big (a new course, a rare pet) | ⏸ Hold until you've banked its value |
| You're in the middle of a fast income streak | ⏸ Finish the streak first |
| You're unsure | ✅ Lean yes — early rebirths are cheap |

New players over-delay their first rebirths because the reset feels like losing progress. It isn't: the multiplier is the progress. One creator's summary of the loop — "death is not the end, it is your power up" — applies here in spirit: the reset *is* the upgrade.

## What should I do before rebirthing?

Three habits make rebuilds fast:

1. **Redeem codes first.** Code pets like the free Lizard ([codes page](/codes/all-codes/)) earn cash while you rebuild — a permanent income floor that survives every reset.
2. **Keep training efficient.** Training potions and a sensible speed target per course ([speed guide](/guides/speed-guide/)) shrink the rebuild window.
3. **Protect your keepers.** Mutated pets ([mutations guide](/guides/mutations-guide/)) and levelled pets carry their value through every reset — they are the assets that make each rebuild faster than the last.
4. **Rebirth on a rhythm, not a whim.** If your last three sessions all ended with a slow grind, your rebirth timing is late — take the next one earlier.

## How does rebirth fit the bigger loop?

The full cycle, with rebirth as the accelerant:

```
Train speed → win races → eggs → pets → cash ─┐
     ▲                                         │
     └──── rebirth multiplies the cash ◄───────┘
```

Every lap through that loop is faster than the last, and the multiplier is why.
