Race for Eggs is a racing-and-collecting simulator with a tight loop: **train → race → hatch → earn → train again**. The first session decides how fast everything after it goes. This guide is the shortest path through that first hour, based on the official game description and the major creator guides from September 2026.

## What should I do first?

**Redeem the RELEASE code before anything else.** It grants a free **Lizard pet**, and pets earn cash over time — so the Lizard starts paying you during your very first session, before you have won a single race. The code is entered in the Shop menu ([codes page](/codes/all-codes/) has the exact steps).

With the Lizard placed, you have an income floor. Everything else builds on it.

## Speed or races — what comes first?

Alternate, and understand the rule that connects them: **carrying an egg slows you down.** Your usable speed is the speed that wins a race *while holding the egg*. That gives a clear early target:

1. **Train speed** until a course looks winnable while carrying.
2. **Win that race** — placement is what earns the egg, not finishing.
3. **Hatch the egg** into a new pet and place it for more income.
4. **Train again** — now with two pets paying the bills.

Over-training before racing is the most common early mistake: speed only pays off once it converts into eggs. Over-racing without training is the other one — you'll plateau on courses your speed can't hold.

## What should I spend cash on?

The shop sells acceleration, and early priorities are:

| Priority | Buy | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Pet slots** | More placed pets = more income running at once |
| 2 | **Training potions** | Directly speed up the stat that wins races |
| 3 | **Cash potions** | Multiply income — worth it once pets are earning well |
| 4 | **Exclusive packs** | Tiered bundles; treat as a bonus, not the path |

Roblox Plus accounts get **+10% cash** according to the official description — if you already have Plus, that compounds quietly on everything.

## When should I rebirth?

**Rebirth multiplies your cash earnings**, and the multiplier climbs steeply — creator footage from a "starting over" run shows multipliers reaching **512× the base cash** at high rebirth counts. That is the engine that makes late-game pet income absurd.

The timing rule of thumb: **rebirth when rebuilding will be faster than continuing the current grind.** Early on that is sooner than you'd think — the first rebirths come quickly, and each one makes the next run faster. Don't over-optimize; a rebirth you take today usually beats a perfect one you delay.

## What's the long-term goal?

The chase, in order:

1. **Build the cash engine** — common pets, levelled and mutated, paying for speed.
2. **Push course difficulty** — rarer eggs appear on harder tracks, but also on easy ones you can re-run, so don't ignore familiar courses.
3. **Secret pets** — secret eggs and max-level secrets (a Kraken, a dragon egg secret) are the end-game targets the community's biggest videos cover.
