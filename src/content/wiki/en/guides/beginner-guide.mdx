Race for Eggs is a racing-and-collecting simulator with a tight loop: **train → race → hatch → earn → train again**. The first session decides how fast everything after it goes. This guide is the shortest path through that first hour — and the decisions that matter after it.

## What should I do first?

**Redeem the RELEASE code before anything else.** It grants a free **Lizard pet**, and pets earn cash over time — so the Lizard starts paying you during your very first session, before you have won a single race. The code is entered in the Shop menu ([codes page](/codes/all-codes/) has the exact steps).

With the Lizard placed, you have an income floor. Everything else builds on it.

## Speed or races — what comes first?

Alternate, and understand the rule that connects them: **carrying an egg slows you down.** Your usable speed is the speed that wins a race *while holding the egg*. That gives a clear early target:

1. **Train speed** until a course looks winnable while carrying.
2. **Win that race** — placement is what earns the egg, not finishing.
3. **Hatch the egg** into a new pet and place it for more income.
4. **Train again** — now with two pets paying the bills.

Over-training before racing is the most common early mistake: speed only pays off once it converts into eggs. Over-racing without training is the other one — you'll plateau on courses your speed can't hold. The full speed system is in the [speed guide](/guides/speed-guide/).

## What should I spend cash on?

The shop sells acceleration, and early priorities are:

| Priority | Buy | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Pet slots** | More placed pets = more income running at once |
| 2 | **Training potions** | Directly speed up the stat that wins races |
| 3 | **Cash potions** | Multiply income — worth it once pets are earning well |
| 4 | **Exclusive packs** | Tiered bundles; treat as a bonus, not the path |

**Shop tiers** stock progressively better stock — creator footage shows tier 4 carrying turkey eggs, sphinx eggs, snakes and the Rainbow Dog. Treat high tiers as shortcuts once your income supports them, not as the main route: [racing is the engine](/pets/all-eggs/), the shop is the accelerator.

Roblox Plus accounts get **+10% cash** according to the official description — if you already have Plus, that compounds quietly on everything.

## How do the worlds work?

The game is split into **worlds** — world one, world two, and beyond — and each gates its own courses and egg pools (the T-Rex egg shows up in world two). The progression rule:

> **Move up a world when you can win its courses while carrying an egg — not when you can barely scrape through.**

A player farming world one consistently out-earns a player who barely survives world two. Rushing worlds is the second most common progression mistake.

## When should I rebirth?

**Rebirth multiplies your cash earnings**, and the multiplier climbs steeply — creator footage shows multipliers reaching **512× the base cash** at high rebirth counts. That is the engine that makes late-game pet income absurd.

The timing rule of thumb: **rebirth when rebuilding will be faster than continuing the current grind.** Early on that is sooner than you'd think — one creator's run shows five rebirths in quick succession during a rebuild. Don't over-optimize; a rebirth you take today usually beats a perfect one you delay. Full detail in the [rebirth guide](/guides/rebirth-guide/).

## What's the long-term goal?

The chase, in order:

1. **Build the cash engine** — common pets, levelled and mutated, paying for speed ([pets guide](/pets/all-pets/)).
2. **Push egg quality** — rarer eggs appear on harder courses *and* on easy ones you can re-run, so don't ignore familiar tracks ([eggs guide](/pets/all-eggs/)).
3. **Hunt mutations** — gold, radioactive and rainbow variants are where duplicates become assets ([mutations guide](/guides/mutations-guide/)).
4. **Secret pets** — secret eggs and max-level secrets (a Kraken, a dragon egg secret) are the end-game targets.
